The body doesn’t fail. It speaks.

From childhood we learn a single language for understanding illness: the language of the diagnosis, the prescription, the expert who knows more than you do about what is happening in your body. What no one told you is that your body speaks many languages.

Every symptom is a word. Every illness is a sentence the body has been trying to say for a long time. This book gathers more than ten years of clinical practice to help you read them, through the real stories of real people.

It isn’t a book against anything. It is a book for a medicine that is more complete, more human and more curious. One that, besides asking what do you have?, dares to ask what happened to you?

The method

What is Webbing?

Webbing isn’t a single therapy. It is nine disciplines working together, reading the body from different angles to reach the root of what a single approach couldn’t resolve.

They come from very different places: ancient Chinese medicine, biology, psychology, energy fields, plants. And they arrive at the same place.

A web. A weave. A map built from many angles at once.

  1. Traditional Chinese Medicine

    Thousands of years mapping the relationship between emotions and organs: each organ with its emotion, its element and its meridian.

  2. Biomagnetism

    Pairs of magnets placed on precise points of the body to rebalance pH and the energy field. Each pair corresponds to an organ, an emotion and a possible microorganism.

  3. Microbioenergetics

    The symbolic and emotional code of microbes. When it is identified and worked through, the terrain that sustained them changes.

  4. Bioenergetics

    The memories recorded in the tissues. An emotion held for years leaves a physical mark.

  5. Germanic New Medicine

    Each symptom as the biological translation of a conflict the brain registered as a threat.

  6. Biodecoding

    The symbolic language of the symptom: what the affected organ means, and the place that hurts.

  7. Emotional release

    The Emotion Code and other tools for letting go of what was recorded in the body without our knowing.

  8. Bach Flowers

    Thirty-eight flower remedies, each for a specific emotional state. They work on the terrain that sustains the symptom.

  9. Herbalism

    The medicine of plants, supporting the physical body while the other disciplines work on the root.

The author

Paola Gutiérrez Martí

10+years of clinical practice
5,000therapy sessions
9disciplines in one method

Paola didn’t come to alternative medicine out of intellectual curiosity. She came because her own body spoke to her, and she had the courage to listen.

Guadalajara, Mexico, is her city and her base. Her practice has led her to walk alongside people who came after exhausting other options, who no longer knew where else to turn.

She trained in Traditional Chinese Medicine, Biomagnetism, Microbioenergetics, Germanic New Medicine, Bioenergetics, Bach Flowers and Herbalism, studies that took her to Guadalajara, San Francisco, San Diego and Miami. Her teachers include Dr. Isaac Goiz Durán, discoverer of the Biomagnetic Pair, Dr. David Goiz Martínez and Dr. Miguel Ojeda Ríos, creator of Microbioenergetics.

That is how Webbing was born. Not in a classroom, but case by case, question by question, in the daily work of her practice.
